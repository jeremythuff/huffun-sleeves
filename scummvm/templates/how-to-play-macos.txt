{{title}} - plays with ScummVM {{rt.scummvm.version}}

1. Double-click "Play {{title}}.command".
   If macOS says it cannot be opened because it came from the internet:
   - macOS 14 or earlier: Control-click it, choose Open, then Open again.
   - macOS 15 or later: try once, then open System Settings >
     Privacy & Security and click "Open Anyway".
   - Any version: in Terminal, type bash and a space, drag the file into
     the window, and press Return.
2. Your saves are kept in the "saves" folder next to it.

game/ holds the game's original files from your huFFuη archive, unchanged.
