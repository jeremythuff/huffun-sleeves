{{title}}

Open "Play {{title}}.command". It plays the Windows release of Fallout 2 natively on this Mac with Fallout II Community Edition {{rt.fallout2ce.version}}, an open-source rebuild of the original engine. It reads the game's own data files from the game folder; nothing is emulated.

Saves are in game/data/SAVEGAME, so keep the folder together. Settings (screen size, windowed or full screen) are in the game's own .ini and .cfg files in the game folder.

If macOS says it cannot verify "Play {{title}}.command", run it through bash once instead: in Terminal, type bash and a space, drag "Play {{title}}.command" into the window, and press Return. Or approve it in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway.
